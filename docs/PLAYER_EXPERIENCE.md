# NeuroNova: curiosity, clarity and progress

## Goal
Make all six games welcoming, easy to operate with a thumb, and satisfying to learn. Colour alone cannot guarantee engagement; these are design hypotheses to validate through real player feedback, not clinical or cognitive-improvement claims.

## This release
- An ink navy background reduces visual clutter. Mint identifies the main action, warm gold identifies points and achievements, and six consistent accents identify the games. Shapes, labels and checks accompany colour cues.
- Keep the six compact icon cards in a stable 3 × 2 arrangement on phones. Put the main Play / Continue action at the bottom, within easy reach. The last game is remembered locally. A date-based Today's pick invites variety without moving cards or hiding any games.
- Show a quiet checkpoint track in the game header and a five-level milestone on solo results. Goals are visible, attainable and repeat indefinitely. Score rules and saved totals do not change.
- Give constructive, game-specific advice after a round. Brief animation respects the system's reduced-motion setting. Players retain control over starting rounds and multiplayer handoff.
- Pattern Portal, Number Forge and Word Quest get consistent, responsive two-column answer cards. Focus Dash gets a distinct target card and clearer selected states. Echo Grid and Path Recall get stronger tile states and visible selection / route progress.
- Keep multiplayer seats, per-game points and difficulty, zero-second manual previews, and both automatic / manual handoffs intact.

## Why these choices
Competence: make progress and next goals understandable. Autonomy: keep game choice, difficulty, timing and pace under player control. Curiosity: suggest another game and show the next challenge without locking content. Usability: stable positions, generous touch targets and readable contrast reduce avoidable frustration.

No extra currencies, inflated points, forced timers, streak-loss penalties or hidden analytics are introduced. Existing device-local progress is retained. Do not promise that a colour makes everyone stay or that playing treats a medical condition.

## Validation
Check complete rounds and saved progress, all six games on small mobile screens, both multiplayer handoff modes, and reduced-motion rendering. Ask players whether the next action is clear, challenges feel fair, and they want another round. Further changes should follow that feedback.
